Who I Am

My name is Desiree. I’m a Junior Hybrid UX/UI Designer and Front-End Developer, gamer, and tech-support specialist based in Calgary, Alberta. I built RastaAsian Sanctuary because I know what it feels like to be overwhelmed, dismissed, or rushed when you’re just trying to learn or get help.

I learned tech, design, and problem solving by doing it on my own — not because it was easy, but because I had to figure things out when no one else could help. Over time, that turned into a strength. I became patient, clear, and good at breaking things down in a way that makes people feel safe instead of embarrassed.

I built this Sanctuary as a place where people can breathe. A place where learning feels calm. A place where design feels personal. A place where tech support feels human.

A sanctuary isn’t a physical place — it’s a feeling. The moment you exhale because you know you won’t be judged, rushed, or talked down to.
